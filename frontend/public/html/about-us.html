About the Department

HZSITD.

The Hadiya Zone Science and Information Technology Department (HZSITD) is a government body established under the Southern Nations, Nationalities, and Peoples' Region (SNNPR) administration. Our mandate is to plan, coordinate, and oversee science and information technology development activities across all woredas and city administrations within the Hadiya Zone. We are committed to building a digitally empowered and transparent governance framework.

HZSITD operates through seven specialized directories: Software Development & Administration; ICT Basic Development & Expansion; Science, Technology, Creation, Support, Control & Capacity Building; Human Resource Development; Plan, Budget, Preparation, Control, Evaluation & Audit; Record & Center Administration; and the Behavioral Officer function. Together, these directories deliver the department's vision of a fully digitized, accountable, and service-oriented government.
