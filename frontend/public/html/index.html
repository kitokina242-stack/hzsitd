08
FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our department's services, the digital letter system, staff portal access, and ICT support.

The department provides ICT infrastructure development, digital document management, staff capacity building, science and technology promotion, network expansion, and the Letter Paper Sending Portal for inter-office communication.

Authorized staff can access the Letter Portal by logging in with their department credentials via the "Staff Login" button. Contact your directory administrator if you require access credentials.

Every letter submitted through the portal is assigned a unique tracking number. You can monitor its status — including delivery confirmations, forwarding actions, and read receipts — from your Letter Portal dashboard.

The internal staff portal is available to all authorized personnel of the Hadiya Zone Science and Information Technology Department. Access is granted by directory administrators and follows role-based permission controls.

Technical issues can be reported via the Contact page or by emailing support@hzsitd.gov.et. The Software Development and Administration Directory provides ongoing technical support during business hours.
