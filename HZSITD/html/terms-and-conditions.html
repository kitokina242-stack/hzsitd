Understand the Rules & Guidelines for Using HZSITD Digital Services

Terms of Use

These Terms of Use (“Terms”) govern access to and use of the digital platforms, portals, and online services provided by the Hadiya Zone Science and Information Technology Department (“HZSITD”). By accessing the HZSITD Staff Portal, Letter Paper Sending Portal, or related directories and digital services, users agree to comply with and be bound by these Terms.

Capitalized terms shall have the meanings set forth in Section 1, or in the section where they are first defined.

1. Definitions

1.1 “Portal” means the HZSITD digital services platform, including the Staff Portal, the Letter Paper Sending Portal, and departmental administrative systems.

1.2 “User” means any authorized government staff member, administrative officer, or approved stakeholder provisioned with access credentials to the Portal.

1.3 “Directory” means the official public and internal listings of zonal departments, administrative personnel, contact points, and public service resources maintained by HZSITD.

1.4 “Letter” means any official dispatch, administrative memorandum, correspondence record, or attached documentation transmitted through the Letter Paper Sending Portal.

1.5 “Content” means all official correspondence, documents, data records, forms, communications, and media processed, stored, or transmitted through HZSITD digital systems.

1.6 “Services” means the digital tools, automated workflows, document tracking systems, communication channels, and information repositories managed by HZSITD.

2. Acceptable Use

2.1 Authorized Use Only Access to the HZSITD Portal is granted strictly for official governmental operations, administrative correspondence, and authorized public service delivery. Users may access only those systems and data records corresponding to their assigned duties and role-based permissions.

2.2 Prohibition of Unauthorized Access Users shall not attempt to bypass authentication mechanisms, access restricted administrative modules, alter system audit logs, or intercept communications not addressed to their office or role.

2.3 Account Security Each User is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their portal credentials. Sharing accounts, passwords, or authentication tokens is strictly prohibited. Any suspected credential compromise must be reported immediately to HZSITD system administrators.

2.4 Data Handling Responsibilities Users are obligated to ensure the accuracy, confidentiality, and integrity of official letters and attached files uploaded to or processed through the Portal. Dissemination of confidential government documents to unauthorized parties is strictly forbidden.

2.5 Consequences of Misuse Non-compliance with these Terms of Use, misuse of digital resources, or intentional breaches of system security will result in immediate suspension or revocation of portal privileges, administrative disciplinary procedures, and potential legal action under applicable Ethiopian laws and civil service regulations.
