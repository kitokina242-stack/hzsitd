Description

The Public Service Information Portal is a centralized digital gateway designed to modernize public administration and enhance citizen engagement across the Hadiya Zone. The platform streamlines access to government services, official guidelines, and administrative procedures, ensuring transparency, accessibility, and efficiency for all residents.

Phase 1: Citizen Service Directory

Phase 1 establishes a comprehensive, searchable catalog of all public services offered by zonal and woreda sector offices. Citizens can easily review eligibility requirements, necessary documentation, fee schedules, processing timelines, and responsible department contacts.

Phase 2: Online Service Request Submission

Phase 2 introduces automated digital service submission, enabling citizens and businesses to apply for permits, certificates, and government assistance online. The system features end-to-end status tracking, document verification, and SMS notifications.
